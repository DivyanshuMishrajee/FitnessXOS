SELECTED FOCUS

Chest strength

Train pushing strength and shoulder control with movements that fit your experience. Exercises strengthen muscle; they cannot selectively remove fat from the chest.

Training principle

Use controlled repetitions, comfortable range of motion and recovery between sessions. Stop if you feel sharp or unusual pain.

Chest focus

Push-up variations, wall push-ups and general upper-body strength. Chest appearance varies with development, genetics and other factors.

Core & belly

Build core stability with dead bugs, bird dogs and suitable planks. No exercise can target fat loss from just the belly.

Posture & movement

Mobility, back strength and relaxed posture can support comfort and movement—not a guaranteed visual transformation.

GROOMING · PRESENTATION · WELLBEING

Looks & Self-Care Studio

A practical self-care portal for skin, hair, grooming, posture, sleep and personal style. No face scoring, appearance rankings or false promises about changing bone structure or targeting facial fat.

HEALTH-FIRST
◉
FACE & SKIN

Simple skin routine

Keep the routine gentle and consistent. Products should suit your skin and stop if they cause irritation.

  • Wash gently; avoid harsh scrubbing.
  • Use sun protection when outdoors.
  • Keep pillowcases and personal items clean.
✦
HAIR & GROOMING

Hair and basic grooming

Build a low-effort routine around cleanliness, comfort and a style you personally like.

  • Choose a hair-wash routine that suits your scalp.
  • Keep nails and facial hair tidy as preferred.
  • Avoid harsh products that irritate skin.
♧
POSTURE

Posture & presence

Focus on comfortable alignment, mobility and confident communication—not holding a rigid pose all day.

  • Take movement breaks from long sitting.
  • Try gentle upper-back mobility.
  • Practise relaxed, natural standing posture.
⌁
SLEEP & ENERGY

Recovery baseline

Sleep, steady meals and daily movement support energy and general wellbeing. They do not guarantee a particular facial shape.

  • Follow a reasonably consistent sleep window.
  • Get some daylight and everyday movement.
  • Keep hydration regular rather than excessive.
◇
STYLE

Personal style toolkit

Choose comfortable clothes that fit your activities, climate and preferences. There is no single correct look.

  • Prepare a clean, comfortable outfit.
  • Maintain shoes and everyday essentials.
  • Choose a look that feels authentic to you.
＋
FACE PUFFINESS

Understand day-to-day changes

Facial puffiness can vary with sleep, allergies, hydration and other factors. Facial exercises cannot selectively burn facial fat.

  • Log sleep and note unusual changes.
  • Avoid extreme water or salt manipulation.
  • Seek medical advice for sudden or persistent swelling.
